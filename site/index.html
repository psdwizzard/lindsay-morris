About the author

I’m Lindsay Morris, a writer and performer based in Richmond, Virginia. My work spans short fiction, stage plays, sketch, and live storytelling, with a particular interest in complicated characters, uncomfortable situations, and the strange places where comedy and vulnerability overlap.

My writing has been published in Intrinsick Magazine and Santa Clara Review, and my work has been featured in New York, New Orleans, Chicago, and Richmond. I’m currently active at Coalition Theater in Richmond, following several years of writing, editing, and performing with theater companies in Chicago and New Orleans.
